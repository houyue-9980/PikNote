# 拾记 PikNote 1.2.0

这一版主要是“手感”：以前很多地方一点就硬切，现在加了一点点动画，变化更顺。动画都很短（0.15–0.25 秒），不拖慢操作；系统设置里打开“辅助功能 → 显示 → 减弱动态效果”时，全部自动关掉。

## 改进

- **格式工具栏**：鼠标移上去有淡淡的底色，按下轻轻一缩；加粗、列表等按钮的选中状态渐变出现，光标移动时不再一闪一闪；按钮稍微加大，更好点中
- **顶部提示条**：从上面滑下来，右边多了 ✕ 可以直接关掉，8 秒后自己收起（鼠标停在上面时不收）
- **“编辑中… / 已保存”**：切换时淡入淡出
- **书架**：鼠标移到图标上，图标轻轻浮起；拖动、移进归档、新建或删除笔记时，其他图标平滑让位
- **改图标和底色**：换表情、换底色时，预览图标轻轻弹一下，颜色渐变过去
- **大纲**：当前章节的高亮会滑到新位置，不再一格一格地跳
- **主题切换**：浅色 / 深色 / 护眼之间切换时，页面颜色渐变过去
- **公式快捷栏（⌘⇧M）和双击公式的编辑框**：打开时淡入、轻轻落下，关闭时反过来
- **待办**：勾上时勾选框轻轻弹一下
- **悬停效果**：书架、书架管理、大纲、侧栏底部按钮等的悬停底色都改成渐变
- **键盘导航**：在系统设置里打开“键盘导航”后，用 Tab 切到工具栏等按钮时有清楚的蓝色焦点框

# PikNote 1.2.0

This release is about feel: many things used to switch instantly, and now they move a little. Animations are short (0.15–0.25 s) and never slow you down, and they turn off automatically when “Reduce motion” is on in System Settings → Accessibility → Display.

## Improved

- **Formatting bar**: buttons show a soft highlight on hover and press in slightly when clicked; active states (bold, lists, …) fade in instead of flickering as the cursor moves; buttons are a little larger and easier to hit
- **Message bar**: slides down from the top, has a ✕ to close it, and hides itself after 8 seconds (not while you hover it)
- **“Editing… / Saved”** crossfades
- **Bookshelf**: icons lift slightly on hover; other icons glide out of the way when you drag, archive, add or delete notes
- **Icon and color editing**: the preview icon bounces and its color fades when you change it
- **Outline**: the current-section highlight slides to its new place
- **Theme switching**: page colors fade between Light, Dark and Sepia
- **Formula bar (⌘⇧M) and the formula editor**: fade and drop in when opened, and reverse when closed
- **To-dos**: the checkbox pops when you check an item
- **Hover highlights** on the bookshelf, Manage Shelves, outline and sidebar buttons now fade in
- **Keyboard navigation**: with “Keyboard navigation” on in System Settings, toolbar and other buttons show a clear blue focus ring when you Tab to them
